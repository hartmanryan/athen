# Templates — Email, Text, and Ad Copy

All templates use a conversational, first-person tone. This is intentional — personal-sounding messages dramatically outperform corporate templates for real estate lead nurture. Customize the bracketed [placeholders] for each agent's niche, market, and personality.

---

## Facebook Ad Copy Template

**Primary Text:**
```
Are you dreaming of waking up to water views every morning?

I've put together a FREE list of every waterfront home currently for sale in [City/Area] — including off-market properties most buyers never see.

Drop your info below and I'll send it straight to your inbox. No spam, no pressure. Just the list.
```

**Headline:** See All [Niche] Homes for Sale in [City]

**Call to Action:** Get Quote / Learn More / Sign Up

**Adaptation notes:**
- Replace "water views" with the niche's aspirational hook (e.g., "riding your horses on your own land", "living on the fairway")
- Replace "waterfront home" with the specific niche property type
- Keep the tone casual and direct — avoid corporate language
- "Including off-market properties" adds exclusivity and urgency

## Initial Delivery Email Template

Use this when a new lead opts in. Send immediately (automated via Zapier or manually).

**Subject Line:** Here's that list you requested!

**Body:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Thanks so much for reaching out — I'm excited to help!

Here's the [Waterfront Home / Horse Property / Luxury Home] list I put together for [City/Area]:

[INSERT LINK TO YOUR ASSET]

This list is updated regularly, so new properties will appear as they hit the market. I'll keep sending you updates so you're always in the loop.

Quick question — are you looking for something specific? Like a minimum acreage, a certain price range, or a particular area? I'd love to narrow this down for you so I'm sending you exactly what you're looking for.

Talk soon,
[Your Name]
[Your Phone Number]
[Your Brokerage]
```

**Why this template works:**
- Delivers the asset immediately (fulfills the ad promise)
- First-person, casual language feels like a real human wrote it
- Open-ended question at the end invites a reply
- Getting replies improves email deliverability

## Follow-Up Email Templates

### Update Email (send every 2-4 weeks)

**Subject Line variations:**
- "New listings added this week!"
- "3 new [waterfront/horse/luxury] homes just listed"
- "[Month] [niche] market update for [City]"
- "You need to see this new listing"

**Body:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Just wanted to let you know — there are some new [niche property type] listings in [City/Area] that you might love.

Here's the updated list:
[INSERT LINK TO YOUR ASSET]

Anything catching your eye? I'm happy to get you more details or set up a showing on any of these.

[Your Name]
[Your Phone Number]
```

## Text Message Templates

### Initial Text (send within minutes of lead opt-in)

```
Hey [First Name]! This is [Your Name], the local real estate agent. I just sent you the [waterfront home / horse property] list — did it come through okay? Happy to help you find exactly what you're looking for!
```

### Day 3 Follow-Up

```
Hey [First Name], just checking in — did you get a chance to look at the list? Any questions?
```

### Day 7 Follow-Up

```
[First Name], a couple new [waterfront/horse] properties just hit the market. Want me to send details?
```

### Day 14+ Monthly Check-In

```
Hey [First Name], hope you're doing well! The [niche] market in [City] has been [active/shifting/heating up] lately. Let me know if you want an update on what's available.
```

### Address Request Text (for thanks.io mailing list)

```
Hey [First Name]! I put together a monthly [City] [niche] market update with recent sales, new listings, and price trends. I'd love to mail you a copy — what address should I send it to?
```

## Postcard Content Template (thanks.io)

### Monthly Market Update Postcard

**Front:**
- Headline: "[City] [Niche] Market Update — [Month] [Year]"
- Hero image: Beautiful photo of a niche property
- Agent photo, name, phone, and brokerage logo

**Back:**
- 2-3 key market stats:
  - Active listings in niche: [number]
  - Average price: [price]
  - Average days on market: [number]
- 1-2 recent notable sales (address, price, days on market)
- Call to action: "Want to see what's available? Text me at [phone] or visit [link]"
- Agent contact info + QR code linking to property list

**Design tips:**
- Use thanks.io's Real Estate Postcards library as a starting point
- Keep it clean and scannable — not text-heavy
- Update stats monthly so each card has fresh data
- 6x9 size is recommended (good visibility, reasonable cost)
